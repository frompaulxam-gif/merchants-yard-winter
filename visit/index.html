What’s on

A few things
before you visit.

When can I visit?+

Our winter public programme runs on Saturdays. Après Afternoons are 4pm to 6pm from 24 October to 21 November. Brunch is 3pm to 5pm from 28 November to 19 December. Evening events start from 6pm. Check your chosen event and booking for its full timings.

Can I book a table?+

Tables can be reserved for afternoon and brunch sessions through ResDiary, subject to availability. The calendar shows available sittings and their terms. Evening seating is first come, first served.

Do I need an entry ticket?+

Check the event on Fatsoma for the entry options, prices and availability for your chosen date. Booking and entry arrangements can differ by event.

Are walk-ins welcome?+

Walk-ins are welcome subject to space and the entry arrangements for that event. Entry is subject to venue capacity and management discretion.

Is Merchants Yard 18+?+

Yes, Merchants Yard is strictly 18+. Please bring valid photo ID.

What should I wear?+

Think cosy layers and après style. Christmas party outfits are welcome too. No tracksuits or football tops, please.

Can I hire the tipi?+

Yes. The Winter Tipi is available for exclusive private hire outside our Saturday public events. Send us your date, guest numbers and event idea.

What about access or dietary requirements?+

Contact the team before your visit so they can help with your specific needs. Ask bar staff about ingredients and allergens before ordering.
