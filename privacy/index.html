Your privacy.

A clear guide to the services used by this website.

Browsing this website

This website preview does not run advertising pixels or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes requests under GitHub’s privacy notice. A preference for turning background motion off may be saved in your browser on this device.

Bookings and tickets

The booking calendar loads ResDiary only when you choose to open it. Event booking links open the official Merchants Yard reservations section. These services have their own privacy notices and handle the information you provide to them.

Enquiries and updates

The private hire form prepares an email in your own email app. It does not send or store an enquiry on this website. Mailing-list signup opens the existing official Merchants Yard form, where you can review the details before submitting.

Contact

For questions about your information, contact merchantsyardlc@gmail.com.
